RUNS IN YOUR BROWSER

Inspect comma-separated data and preview JSON without converting your strings. Leading zeros stay intact. All parsing happens on this device; the tool sends no data and uses no analytics or external libraries.

First row = headers. Commas only. Quoted commas, quoted newlines, escaped quotes, CRLF, and a UTF-8 BOM are supported. Text is never evaluated. Maximum 2 MB of UTF-8 input.
